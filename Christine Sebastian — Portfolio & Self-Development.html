About Me

A mix of structured code, creativity, and honest reflection

I'm a first-year BSCS student who loves pairing analytical problem-solving with visual storytelling. Transitioning into college brings new routines, unfamiliar platforms like Canvas, and deeper questions about how I learn, rest, and connect with others.

Through Self-Development, I've started looking at my habits with more patience—recognizing where I tend to delay or overthink, leaning into my resilience, and taking practical steps forward one week at a time.

  • Hidden Superpower Resilience and figuring things out—staying persistent and learning what's needed even when tasks feel unfamiliar.
  • Current Boss Battle Procrastination and overthinking—putting tasks off after resting while worrying about accuracy and deadlines.
  • Intentional Action Using free time right away to start tasks instead of waiting for a "perfect" moment, and trusting the process.
Self-Development Reflection

Learning growth & evolving habits

Reflections on how my perspective, study habits, and emotional awareness have grown in Self-Development.

01 • Self-Discovery

Embracing Introversion & Connection

Starting out as a shy, introverted first-year student on an unfamiliar campus felt daunting at first. I've come to see that feeling a bit quiet around new people is normal, while staying genuinely open to making friends and building meaningful connections.

02 • Habit & Focus Shifts

Moving Past Procrastination & Worry

Learning in Self-Development helped me notice how delaying work after a break only adds stress later—especially while adjusting to Canvas and wanting every assignment done right. Starting small tasks promptly and focusing on what I can control helps calm that cycle.

03 • Mindset & Resilience

Optimism, Boundaries & Trusting the Process

Rather than expecting to have everything figured out right away, I'm practicing active optimism, setting clearer boundaries so I don't over-accommodate others at my own expense, and growing both academically and personally at a steady pace.

Self-Assessment Insights

Strengths, traits, learning style & growth plan

Key takeaways synthesized from my VIA character strengths, personality traits, mind map, and character profile.

Top VIA Strengths & Core Anchor

Grounded in honesty, love, spirituality, hope, and forgiveness.

  • Personal Statement: Using my strengths to grow better by staying honest, hopeful, and faithful through challenges while supporting others.
  • Character Anchor ("CORIS"): Strong patience and dedication when learning topics I care about, helping teammates by guiding them patiently and respectfully.
  • Level-Up Move: Channeling focus into what supports my growth and tuning out distractions that pull me off track.

Personality Traits & Learning Style

How I process ideas, collaborate, and recharge.

  • Personality Traits: High openness (curious and creative), low neuroticism (calm and resilient), high agreeableness (empathetic and peace-seeking), balanced conscientiousness (responsible and flexible), and introverted extraversion (recharging in solitude while valuing deep bonds).
  • Learning Style: Visual and textual learner—grasping concepts best through diagrams, flowcharts, written notes, and hands-on practice.
  • Growth Plan: Study actively with mind maps and practice questions, clarify group expectations early, and reframe progress with hope.
